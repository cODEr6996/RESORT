01 — THE IDEA

Stay close to nature.
Leave a lighter footprint.

Mist Wood Haven is an imaginary jungle resort concept designed around Sikkim's forests, mountains and quiet atmosphere.

Instead of treating nature as a backdrop, the resort makes it part of the guest experience. Villas, trails, recreation and dining are planned to work with the landscape while encouraging responsible tourism.

05 — NATURE FIRST

A resort that
works with nature.

The concept prioritizes low-impact planning, resource conservation and respectful guest behaviour.

01

Minimal Land Disturbance

Work around existing vegetation and natural contours wherever possible.

02

Water Conservation

Rainwater harvesting, low-flow fixtures and responsible landscaping.

03

Cleaner Energy

Solar power can support selected lighting and common-area requirements.

04

Responsible Waste

Separate waste, reduce single-use plastics and compost suitable organic waste.

05

Wildlife Respect

No feeding or disturbing wildlife, marked trails and controlled night lighting.

GUEST PLEDGE

Leave only footprints.

  • Carry reusable bottles
